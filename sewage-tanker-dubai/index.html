Sewage and wastewater removal, handled properly

Our tankers collect sewage, septic waste and wastewater from villas, residential buildings, labour accommodation, construction sites, restaurants, warehouses and industrial units. Every load goes to an authorised disposal point, so you're never exposed to fines for improper disposal.

Who we work with

  • Villas and buildings on septic tanks or without a sewer connection
  • Construction sites and labour camps
  • Restaurants, hotels and commercial kitchens
  • Warehouses, factories and industrial areas
  • Facilities management companies running multiple sites

One-off pickups or a scheduled contract

Need a single tank emptied? Call us and we'll dispatch a tanker. If your tank fills regularly, a monthly contract puts it on a fixed collection schedule so it never overflows. That's ideal for camps, sites and buildings that aren't connected to the sewer network.

How it works

  1. Tell us the location, the approximate tank size and how soon you need it.
  2. We confirm the price and send the right size tanker.
  3. Our crew empties the tank and leaves the area clean.
  4. The waste is taken to an authorised disposal facility.
