What sweet water is used for

Sweet water is fresh, low-salinity water. It's the right choice for filling swimming pools, topping up villa and building water tanks, and for construction work such as concrete mixing and curing.

  • Swimming pool filling and top-ups
  • Villa and building water tanks
  • Construction sites: concrete mixing, curing and site use
  • Landscaping, gardens and vehicle washing
  • Events and temporary sites

Swimming pool filling

Filling a pool from a garden hose can take days and push up your water bill. A sweet water tanker fills it in one visit. Tell us the pool size and we'll work out how much water you need.

Daily or monthly supply for sites

Construction and industrial sites that use water every day can set up a scheduled supply. We deliver on the days you need, so work never stops waiting for water.

How it works

  1. Tell us where the water is going and roughly how much you need.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a sweet water tanker cost in Dubai?

The price depends on how much water you need, your location and the delivery time. Share those details and we'll quote you upfront.

How much water do I need to fill my pool?

Multiply length × width × average depth in metres to get cubic metres. One cubic metre is 1,000 litres (about 220 imperial gallons). Send us your pool size and we'll calculate it for you.

Is sweet water the same as drinking water?

No. Sweet water is fresh water for pools, tanks and construction. If you need water that's fit to drink, see our drinking water supply.
