Regular supply for camps and sites

Accommodation and sites that rely on tanker water need it to arrive on time, every time. We schedule deliveries around your consumption so storage tanks never run dry.

Potable water or sweet water?

Potable water is water that's fit to drink. Sweet water is fresh water for pools, tanks and construction and isn't intended for drinking. If you're not sure which you need, tell us what the water is for and we'll advise. For pools and site use, see our sweet water tanker service.

How it works

  1. Tell us the location, the volume and how often you need it.
  2. We confirm the price and a delivery time or schedule.
  3. The tanker fills your storage tank on site.
