Why contractors use salt water

Many earthworks don't need fresh water. Where the project specification allows it, using salt water for soil compaction, backfilling and piling keeps costs down and saves fresh water for where it's really needed.

  • Soil compaction and backfilling
  • Piling works
  • Road and infrastructure earthworks
  • Site preparation and groundworks

Supply that keeps pace with your site

Groundworks get through a lot of water. We set up daily or weekly deliveries matched to your programme, and you can call any time to add an extra load.

Frequently asked questions

What is salt water used for in construction?

Mainly soil compaction, backfilling and piling, where the project specification allows it. Check with your consultant if you're unsure.
